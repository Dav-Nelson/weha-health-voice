Ethics / Inclusion Note

Weha Health does not collect names, emails, or identifying information — conversations are linked only to an anonymous device session ID, deletable at any time. All health guidance is explicitly framed as non-diagnostic, with every response closing on a reminder to consult a qualified provider, and emergency-level symptoms are directed to immediate in-person care rather than handled in-app.
Our escalation feature (WhatsApp/Telegram alerts, facility lookup) is a working prototype demonstrating a real agentic pathway, not a connection to live emergency dispatch — this is stated plainly to test users and in the demo video to avoid false expectations of guaranteed emergency response.
Our own benchmark audio samples (Pidgin, Yoruba, Akan, Amharic) were recorded by team members as simulated conversations, not real patients, with explicit recorded verbal consent stated at the start of each clip and no real names or real health information used.
We are transparent about model limitations rather than hiding them: our benchmark documents that AssemblyAI has no native support for our four African languages, and Hugging Face's MMS model has no dedicated Nigerian Pidgin adapter — these are reported as findings, not concealed as failures. This reflects our broader stance that fair benchmarking means showing where models fail African languages, not just where they succeed.
